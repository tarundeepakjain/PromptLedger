## FAQ

**Q: Is this actually free?**
Yes. The audit is completely free. No credit card, no account creation, no trial period. You enter your tools, get your results, and leave — or stay and book a Credex consultation if the savings are significant enough to warrant it.

---

**Q: How accurate are the recommendations?**
Every pricing figure in the audit engine traces back to an official vendor pricing page, verified at submission time. The recommendation logic is capability-aware — it will never suggest a cheaper tool that can't actually handle your stated use case. You can see the reasoning behind every recommendation in the per-tool breakdown.

---

**Q: Does the tool store my data?**
If you choose to receive your audit by email, your email address and company name are stored securely in our database. The shareable audit URL strips all identifying information — tools and savings numbers only. If you don't enter your email, nothing is stored beyond the anonymised audit result used to generate your shareable link.

---

**Q: What tools does PromptLedger support?**
Currently: Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Windsurf, Anthropic API, OpenAI API, and Gemini API. Both subscription and direct API billing models are supported. More tools will be added based on user feedback.

---

**Q: What is Credex, and why does it appear in my results?**
Credex sells discounted AI infrastructure credits — Cursor, Claude, ChatGPT Enterprise, and others — sourced from companies that overforecast their usage. For audits showing more than $500/month in potential savings, we surface Credex as an additional way to capture those savings, beyond the plan optimisations the audit already recommends. There's no obligation to engage with Credex to use the audit tool.
